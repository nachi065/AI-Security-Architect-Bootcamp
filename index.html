    AI Security Architect Bootcamp¶

    A four-volume, self-study programme in designing, testing, governing and defending enterprise AI systems: LLM applications, RAG pipelines and autonomous agents. It follows one running case study through build, break, govern and architect.

    The programme in one picture: each volume ends with a gate, and all four work on the same system.

    Where to begin¶

    • New here, or not sure where to join? Start here has a route for each background and says where each set of rules is.
    • Starting from scratch? Go to Volume 1 and begin with Week 1.
    • Keeping the programme current? The Landscape currency map lists what to re-check each quarter.

    The four volumes¶

    Volume Focus Stage Weeks Hours (est.) Gate output
    Volume 1 Foundations: Build It First Build 13 ~120-130 Foundations Gate, a working RAG application and agent, and a threat model
    Volume 2 Advanced AI Red Teaming Break 8 ~70 A red-team report with an exploit chain and remediation roadmap
    Volume 3 AI Governance, Compliance and Risk Govern 8 ~65 A governance programme pack: policy, risk register, roadmap
    Volume 4 Secure AI Architecture and Engineering Architect 8 ~80 An architecture package and a design-authority defence

    Total: 37 weeks of material. See the study plan for a suggested order.

    How to study¶

    • Work through the weeks in order. Each week has core concepts, a study plan, a guided lab, a deliverable and self-check questions.
    • Do the labs yourself. Commit every deliverable to your own portfolio repository.
    • Tick Mark as complete at the bottom of a week when you have finished the deliverable and answered the self-check questions closed-book.
    • Use the notes box on each week page for your own lab notes.
    • Use search (top right) to find a topic across all four volumes.

    Please read¶

    • Hours are planning estimates from the curriculum, not measured.
    • Regulation, standards and tool syntax change quickly. Each volume lists what to re-check; Volume 3 is not legal advice.
    • This site stores your progress and notes in this browser only. Use the export button above to back them up.
