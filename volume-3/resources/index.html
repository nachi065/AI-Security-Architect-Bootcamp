    Sources, resources and verification notes¶

    Legal and regulatory facts in this guide are current as of 5 October 2026 and were checked only in the way described below. None of this is legal advice.

    Pages I opened in full¶

    • Cloud Security Alliance AI Safety Initiative, "EU AI Act's High-Risk Deadline: Deferred, Not Cancelled" (1 August 2026): basis for the EU AI Act dates, the Omnibus content, Article 50 status and penalty tiers in Week 2. It lists the EUR-Lex entry for Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 as its primary reference; read that text.
    • Morgan Lewis, "UAE Establishes Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data" (15 June 2026): basis for the UAE authority and the PDPL implementing-regulations point in Week 4.

    Seen only as search-result excerpts (not opened in full)¶

    Treat these as leads to verify:

    • Law-firm summaries of the EU AI Omnibus, including K&L Gates (via Mondaq) and Gibson Dunn. They agree on the Annex III and Annex I deferrals; they vary in wording on the transparency grace period.
    • AZB & Partners on India's DPDP phased rollout and King Stubb & Kasiva on 2026 DPDP milestones: basis for the three DPDP dates. They differ by one day on the May 2027 date.
    • CMS Saudi Arabia AI regulation scanner and the Chambers Data Protection and Privacy 2026 guide for Saudi Arabia: basis for the Saudi PDPL and soft-law picture.

    Known conflicts and gaps¶

    • UAE PDPL implementing regulations. The Morgan Lewis note says they had not been issued as of June 2026. A lower-quality web page claims they were issued in 2026. I could not resolve this; check the UAE government and legal sources.
    • EU transparency grace period. Sources differ on which Article 50 sub-obligations have later dates. Read the Official Journal text.
    • Oman, and the Singapore framework edition. Not verified.
    • NIST IR 8596 and the AI Agent Standards Initiative. Statuses come from the curriculum and I did not verify them.
    • ISO/IEC standards. Paid documents. Read them through your organisation or a library, and do not paste their text into your notes.

    Review cadence¶

    The curriculum asks for a quarterly landscape review. For your own use, set a calendar reminder to re-check Weeks 2 to 4 against primary sources every three months, and before any client or interview use.
