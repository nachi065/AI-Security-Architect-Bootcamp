    Landscape Currency Map¶

    AI security standards and regulation change monthly. The status column below records what the sources checked on 5 October 2026 indicated. It is a teaching aid, not legal advice; every row should be re-checked against the primary source. The Volume 3 resources page gives the review cadence.

    Verify before use: Volume 3, Week 4 holds a more detailed snapshot of the regional items (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, India) with a confidence rating per row. Prefer that page for the regional rules, and still confirm against official texts and qualified local counsel.

    Framework / regulation Status and notes Taught in
    OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 Current LLM-specific list in the sources checked; confirm no newer edition before you rely on it. V1 W12; V2 W1–2, 4
    OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026 Published 9 Dec 2025; ten categories covering goal hijack, tool misuse, identity, supply chain, code execution, memory, inter-agent communication, cascading failures, human-agent trust and rogue agents. Extends, does not replace, the LLM list. V1 W13; V2 W6; V4 W5
    OWASP MCP Top 10 Referenced by a vendor source only; verify existence, version and status on the OWASP site before relying on it. V2 W6; V4 W5
    MITRE ATLAS Core adversarial-AI knowledge base. A March 2026 advocacy submission said it lacks coverage of multi-agent lateral movement; re-check current release. V2 W1, 6
    NIST AI 100-2 (adversarial ML taxonomy) 2025 edition referenced in sources checked. V1 W10; V2 W4
    NIST AI RMF 1.0 and AI 600-1 (GenAI profile) Stable reference documents. V3 W3
    NIST IR 8596 Cyber AI Profile Preliminary draft published Dec 2025; a later draft was expected in summer 2026. Verify current draft or final status. V3 W3; V4 W1
    NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative (CAISI) Launched 17 Feb 2026; first AI Agent Interoperability Profile planned for Q4 2026. NCCoE agent identity and authorization concept paper issued Feb 2026. V3 W3; V4 W5
    EU AI Act and Digital Omnibus on AI Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) reported in force 27 Jul 2026. Annex III high-risk duties deferred to 2 Dec 2027; Annex I to 2 Aug 2028; transparency duties reported as still applying from 2 Aug 2026 with transitional nuances. Verify against the Official Journal text. V3 W2
    ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 23894 AI management system and AI risk guidance standards. V3 W3, 7
    Regional: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, India (DPDP) Not independently verified in this revision; confirm current law, rules and guidance before you rely on it. V3 W4
    CSA frameworks (MAESTRO, AI Controls Matrix), CoSAI, Google SAIF Named from general knowledge; confirm current versions before use. V4 W1, 4

    In the "Taught in" column, V1 W12 means Volume 1, Week 12.

    Items to verify¶

    • All third-party links and course durations.
    • OWASP MCP Top 10 existence and version; any newer OWASP LLM Top 10 edition.
    • NIST IR 8596 and AI Agent Interoperability Profile status (both were drafts or planned as of the sources checked).
    • EU AI Act dates against the Official Journal text, especially Article 50 transitional provisions.
    • UAE, Saudi, Omani and Indian regulatory details, and any Singapore agentic-AI governance extension.
    • CSA MAESTRO and AI Controls Matrix current versions; model-file scanner tool names.
    • Hour estimates, which are planning figures: track your own hours and adjust.
